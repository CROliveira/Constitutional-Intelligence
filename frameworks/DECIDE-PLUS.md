# DECIDE-PLUS™ — A Decision Methodology for AI-Era Leaders

## What it is

DECIDE-PLUS is a structured decision methodology for leaders facing high-stakes technical choices — AI adoption, automation of adjudication, major procurements. It forces constitutional evaluation into the decision itself: the decision is not complete until its legal basis, rights implications, evidence quality, and review mechanisms are explicit and documented. The "PLUS" is the accountability surplus most decision processes skip: who answers for this, and when do we revisit it.

## When to use it

- Approving, pausing, or killing an AI initiative.
- Procurement decisions with downstream effects on rights or service equity.
- Any technical decision that could end up scrutinized by oversight, media, or courts.
- Executive committees that need a shared, defensible record of *why* — not just *what*.

## Three-step application

1. **Define the decision and its constitutional context.** State the decision in one sentence. Name the legal authority it rests on, the mandate it serves, and the Charter or statutory rights it could touch. If the authority is unclear, stop — clarify it before proceeding.
2. **Evaluate options against evidence and rights.** Lay out genuine alternatives (including "do nothing" and "do it without AI"). Score each on evidence quality, risk, reversibility, and rights impact using the [CHARTER RIGHTS DECISION TREE™](CHARTER-RIGHTS-DECISION-TREE.md) where applicable. Document what you considered and rejected.
3. **Decide, assign, and schedule review.** Record the decision, the rationale, the accountable owner, and a review trigger (date or event). A decision without a review date is a decision without accountability.
